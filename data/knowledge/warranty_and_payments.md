# Warranty

All electronics, including earbuds, carry a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects. Outerwear and footwear carry a 6-month warranty. The warranty does not cover normal wear, accidents or water damage beyond the product rating.

To make a warranty claim, contact support@shopassist.example or call 1-800-555-0199 with your order id and photos of the defect.

# Payment Methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay. Payment is captured when the order ships. We never ask for your full card number over chat or email.

# Price Matching

We match the price of identical items sold by authorised retailers within 14 days of purchase. Contact support with a link to the lower price.
