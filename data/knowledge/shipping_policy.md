# Shipping Options

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days and is free on orders over $50. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs $14.99. Orders placed before 2 PM Eastern ship the same business day.

# International Shipping

We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom and the European Union. International delivery takes 7-14 business days. Import duties and taxes are paid by the customer on delivery.

# Tracking

A tracking number is emailed once your order ships. You can also ask ShopAssist "where is my order" to get the latest carrier scan.

# Order Changes and Cancellation

You can cancel or change an order within 1 hour of placing it. After the order enters processing it can no longer be cancelled, but you can return it after delivery.
