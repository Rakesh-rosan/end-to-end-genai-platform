# Returns and Refunds

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Final-sale items and opened earbuds cannot be returned for hygiene reasons.

To start a return, open the order in your account and choose "Return item", or ask ShopAssist for a refund with your order id. Return shipping is free for orders over $50.

# Refund timing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Once we receive the returned item, the refund appears within 5-7 business days. Refunds above $200 are reviewed by a support specialist before they are approved.

# Exchanges

Exchanges for a different size or colour are free within 30 days of delivery, subject to stock availability.
